# Excluded Principal: hiding a group cascades to its Member rows, never to an unrelated Direct grant

Excluding (hiding) a Principal matches by identity alone: an entry is hidden if its own `principal.id` is in the excluded-id set, or — the one cascade — it's a `Member` entry whose `accessType.group_id` is excluded. Excluding a group therefore hides the group's own row and every Member row derived from it, but leaves an unrelated Direct (or other Group) grant held by the same person untouched, since that grant is a separate `PrincipalEntry` carrying no reference to the excluded group. The alternative — sweeping up every entry belonging to any user reachable through the excluded group — was considered and rejected: it would silently hide a real, independent grant (e.g. a personal Direct admin grant) that has nothing to do with why the group was excluded, defeating the feature's purpose of suppressing known noise without hiding signal.

This parallels, but is narrower than, the existing `cascadeMemberCount` cascade-warning shown when staging a Group grant's removal (ADR-0022) — that count also stops at Member grants derived from the group being removed, never reaching into a member's unrelated Direct grants.
