## What to build

The two fixed bars at the top of the console: the 40px head with the Run pair selectors, and the 44px summary bar built from PD-17's aggregates.

This is where **Baseline** and **Comparison** replace "Snapshot A / Snapshot B" in the UI, per the `CONTEXT.md` entry added in the design session.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A 40px head bar renders the `perm-diff` wordmark in mono 600 with the `repository access audit` eyebrow, the Baseline selector, a ⇄ swap control, the Comparison selector, and the span note.
- [ ] Both selectors list stored Snapshots as `run N · YYYY-MM-DD` and change the Run pair when picked.
- [ ] The swap control exchanges Baseline and Comparison, and the view updates to match — a pair that showed 4 escalations shows 4 demotions after a swap.
- [ ] The span note reads `N days apart · X → Y grants`, or `single run · X → X grants` when both sides are the same Snapshot.
- [ ] A 44px summary bar renders two clusters: current state (grants, repos, users, admin, write, read) and delta (added, revoked, changed, escalations, repos hit, net), with numerals in mono 600 at 15px and `-0.02em`.
- [ ] Delta figures carry their state hues; `net` shows an explicit sign.
- [ ] When both sides are the same Snapshot, the right cluster renders em-dashes in `--ink-faint` under the label `no baseline — roster only`, and the left cluster still shows real figures.
- [ ] Summary figures come from PD-17 and describe the whole Run pair — they do not change when the view changes.

## Out of Scope

- The 31px filter bar and its controls — PD-20.
- The notice strip — PD-19.
- Persisting the selected Run pair across app restarts.

## Further Notes

The selector labels are the reason `CONTEXT.md`'s `Run pair` entry now names its members by role rather than position: Grant/Revoke/Level change are always movement *from* the Baseline *to* the Comparison, which "A" and "B" never conveyed.
