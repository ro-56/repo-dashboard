## What to build

Widen editing to Group grants at both Repo and Project scope, reusing PD-60/PD-61's pendingEdits state, confirm dialog, Apply control, and scope-dispatch pattern. Adds one genuinely new piece of logic: warning the user how many Member grants will disappear if they remove a Group grant, since Member grants are computed from the Group's own grant rather than stored separately (ADR-0022).

- **`BitbucketClient`**: add repo- and project-scope Group-grant write methods — set/delete at `.../permissions-config/groups/{group_slug}` for both `/2.0/repositories/{workspace}/{repo_slug}/` and `/2.0/workspaces/{workspace}/projects/{project_key}/`.
- **`apply_pending_edits`**: extend routing so `PendingEditRequest`s with `target: Group(group_slug)` reach the new client methods, at either scope.
- **Cascade-count computation**: given the roster tree, count how many `Member` entries derive from a given Group entry at a given repo/project scope. Surface this count both in the Group row's menu (when Remove is being considered) and in the confirm dialog's entry for that staged removal.
- **`rosterRow.ts`**: extend the menu-building function to Group entries at both scopes. Member entries continue to get no menu at all (ADR-0021 — permanent, not a placeholder for a future ticket).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A Group grant row (Repo or Project scope) shows the same Read/Write/Admin + Remove menu as Direct rows.
- [ ] Opening the menu on a Group row about to be removed shows a count of Member grants that derive from it at that repo/project (e.g. "removing this also drops access for 4 members").
- [ ] The confirm dialog shows the same cascade count next to any staged Group removal.
- [ ] A successful apply calls Bitbucket's real repo- or project-level group permissions-config endpoint (set or delete as appropriate).
- [ ] A batch mixing Direct and Group edits across both scopes applies each independently and reports per-item results.
- [ ] Member rows still show no menu at all.
- [ ] `cargo test` covers `apply_pending_edits`'s Group routing at both scopes (set, delete) against a fake `BitbucketClient`.
- [ ] A pure-function test (colocated with wherever the cascade count is computed) covers: a group with 0, 1, and several resolvable members; a group with unresolved membership (should not crash — count is simply unavailable/omitted, consistent with the `Unresolvable membership` glossary entry).
- [ ] Colocated `rosterRow.test.ts` additions cover the Group-entry menu and its cascade-count note.
- [ ] Manually verified via `pnpm tauri dev` that removing a Group grant, once applied and re-run, also removes its Member rows from the roster.
