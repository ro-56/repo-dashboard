## What to build

Delete the structural notice strip (`NoticeStrip.svelte`, `noticeStrip.ts`) entirely, per user decision (a `/grill-with-docs` session): the strip is not useful and should go, not be redesigned further (superseding PD-49's badge-pill work).

Two structural facts it uniquely carried have no replacement surface, by explicit user decision:
- **Workspace departure/arrival**: dropped entirely, including its backend computation (`WorkspaceState` in `src-tauri/src/roster.rs`, `workspaceState` in `src/lib/roster.ts`) and its `CONTEXT.md` glossary entries — the concept no longer exists in the system, not just the UI.
- **Same-Snapshot explainer** ("both sides point at run N..."): dropped, no replacement.

Two facts are *not* lost — Fetch failure and Unresolvable membership keep their existing independent per-row tags (`fetch failed`, `members unresolved`, PD-19), which were always the primary channel; the strip's prose was supplementary.
Repo absence/arrival is also not lost — `repoCard.ts`'s `absentSide()` strike-through/tag already covers it independently.

## Scope

- Delete `src/lib/noticeStrip.ts`, `src/lib/components/NoticeStrip.svelte`; remove usage from `src/routes/dashboard/+page.svelte`.
- Remove `--notice-*` tokens from `tokens.css` (fix header comment).
- Remove `WorkspaceState`/`workspace_state` end-to-end: Rust enum/field/computation + cargo tests in `src-tauri/src/roster.rs`, TS type/field in `src/lib/roster.ts`, fixture usages in `repoCard.test.ts`/`rosterRow.test.ts`.
- Remove Workspace departure/arrival entries from `CONTEXT.md`.
- Edit ADR-0005 (drop "or the notice strip" from its consequence sentence) and add a new ADR recording this deletion.
- Drop `noticeStrip.ts` from the pure-TS-module lists in `CLAUDE.md` and ADR-0017.
