## Context

From the 2026-08-01 architecture review (candidate 2, \"Strong\"): PD-69 extracted the per-repo/per-Project HTTP-fetch and membership-resolution logic (`fetch_repo_permissions`, `fetch_project_permissions`) out of `collect_and_store`'s inlined loop, specifically so Refresh (PD-68/70/71) wouldn't have to duplicate it. That extraction stopped one level too early.

The step that turns one `ProjectFetchResult`'s raw responses into per-repo `PermissionRecord`s — calling `normalize_project_permissions` then `normalize_project_group_permissions` and appending the results to `records`/`group_membership_statuses` — is still copy-pasted almost verbatim in two places:
- `src-tauri/src/collect.rs:258-286` (inside the per-repo loop, guarded by `project_cache`)
- `src-tauri/src/refresh.rs:192-222` (inside the per-Project-target loop, over `repos_by_project`)

Deletion test: a `flatten_project_result_to_repo(project_key, repo, &ProjectFetchResult) -> (Vec<PermissionRecord>, Vec<GroupMembershipStatus>)` helper would delete ~15 duplicated lines in each file with no complexity increase elsewhere — this is a legitimate missed extraction, not over-abstraction speculation.

## What to explore

- Confirm the two call sites' surrounding context (project_cache in collect.rs vs repos_by_project loop in refresh.rs) don't have subtle differences beyond what's copy-pasted — read both blocks side by side before extracting, since a mismatch would explain why PD-69 didn't merge this step already.
- Decide where the new function lives (likely alongside `fetch_project_permissions`, same module) and its exact signature/ownership (does it take owned data or borrow the `ProjectFetchResult`?).

## Acceptance criteria
